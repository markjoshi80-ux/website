About us

Faithful hands for real-life needs.

Faithful Hands & Hope Collective Society serves individuals and families through practical care, trusted relationships, and community-rooted support.

Our mission

We help neighbors move from hardship toward hope.

Our work is grounded in the belief that every person deserves dignity, compassion, and a caring community around them. We respond to immediate needs while helping people connect with resources that support stability.

We serve through food and essentials, street support, learning opportunities, and community care. Each program is designed to meet people with respect and walk alongside them at a human pace.

Faith Guided by compassion and service.
Hope Creating pathways toward stability.
Action Meeting needs with practical care.

Meet Our Founder

Mark Joshi – Founder & Executive Director

For more than 15 years, I built a successful career in the telecommunications industry, helping connect people in an ever-changing world.

Today, I dedicate my time, energy, and heart to serving homeless and displaced individuals in our communities.

As a published author in the field of politics, I have spent years examining the issues that shape our society and advocating for meaningful conversations about change.

Writing has allowed me to explore the challenges facing our communities, but my work on the ground has taught me that lasting change begins with compassion, understanding, and a willingness to walk alongside others on their journey.

At the heart of everything I do is a simple belief: we are all connected. When one person struggles, we all have an opportunity to show kindness. When someone feels forgotten, we can remind them that they matter. When hope seems lost, even the smallest act of compassion can help light the way forward.

My mission is not only to help meet immediate needs, but to restore hope, inspire purpose, and help people rediscover their own strength and worth. I believe every person deserves to be seen, valued, and given the opportunity to build a brighter future.

Whether through community outreach, advocacy, writing, or simply being present for those who need someone to listen, I strive to live a life rooted in compassion, integrity, and service. Above all, I believe that when we lead with love, empathy, and faith in one another, we can build stronger communities and transform lives, one person at a time.

"Truly I tell you, whatever you did for one of the least of these brothers and sisters of mine, you did for me."
-Matthew 25:40

With a passion for helping others and a deep belief in the power of compassion, Kathy plays a vital role in guiding the organization's mission to support vulnerable individuals and families. Her extensive experience in managing teams, building relationships, and creating positive environments has equipped her with the skills needed to lead with both integrity and purpose.

Kathy believes that every person deserves dignity, respect, and the opportunity to thrive. Through her work with Faithful Hands & Hope Collective Society, she is dedicated to making a meaningful difference in the lives of those facing hardship, while helping to build stronger, more connected communities.

Driven by faith, service, and a genuine desire to help others, Kathy continues to inspire positive change through leadership, empathy, and action.

"Together, we can bring hope, restore dignity, and create brighter futures for those who need it most." 💚
